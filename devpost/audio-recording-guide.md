# Wazn audio: recording guide (Phase 3, DRAFT for learner review)

## What to record
1. **Pattern clips first:** one per pattern, said with the ف-ع-ل placeholder letters (فَعَلَ, فَعَّلَ, فاعَلَ, أَفْعَلَ, فاعِل, مَفْعُول, مَفْعَل, فِعالة, then Forms V-X). About 14 clips.
2. **Word clips second:** one per ✓ word in the final word list (about 60 for Phase 1, about 85 with Forms V-X).

## How to record
- **Equipment:** your phone's voice recorder is fine. Use a quiet room with soft furnishings (a wardrobe full of clothes is a classic home studio) and hold the phone about 20 cm away.
- **Delivery:** one take per word, clear and unhurried, with the full vowels pronounced (كاتِب, not a casual كاتب). Leave about half a second of silence before and after.
- **Batching:** record a whole root in one sitting so the voice and volume stay consistent.

## Files
- **Format:** MP3, mono, 64-96 kbps. It plays in every browser and is small enough that ~100 clips stay under about 2 MB in total. If your phone saves `.m4a`, that's fine: the build converts it.
- **Names:** transliterated and lowercase, so the app can find each file without a lookup table:
  - patterns: `pattern-faail.mp3`, `pattern-mafool.mp3`, `pattern-form-2.mp3`
  - words: `ktb-katib.mp3`, `ktb-maktab.mp3`, `drs-madrasa.mp3`
- **Where:** drop them all into one folder. The build sorts out the rest (trimming silence, matching volume).

## Open for your decision
- Your voice only, or a second reader (e.g. a teacher or a native speaker) for authenticity?
- Words in pausal form (كاتِبْ) or with full endings (كاتِبٌ)? Dictionary-style pausal form is the usual choice for learners.
