Amiri, colour-coded: ink letters come from the root, gold letters come from the pattern. Pick a word and press Play.

■ from the root■ from the pattern
  1. The pattern waits in faint gold: its ف ع ل placeholders show where the root letters will go.
  2. Root tiles lift off and fly along an arc, one after another.
  3. On landing the placeholder is knocked out (it floats up and fades), the letter squashes slightly like it has weight, and a gold ink ripple marks the spot.
  4. The letter morphs into its joined shape as it lands (a cross-fade, not a jump).
  5. The finished word settles as real text, colour-coded root vs pattern, and the meaning rises in.
  6. Reduced motion (for people who turn off animations on their device) gets a calm fade instead.
