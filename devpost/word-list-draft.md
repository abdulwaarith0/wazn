## The questions that were asked

1. **The "place" column mixes three shapes:** مَفْعَل (مَكْتَب), مَفْعِل (مَسْجِد, مَجْلِس) and مَفْعَلة (مَدْرَسة, مَحْكَمة, مَزْرَعة). My suggestion is to treat it as one column, "place noun / اسم المكان", and show the exact shape in Grammar mode. Otherwise we lose the best examples (مسجد, مدرسة). Do you agree?
2. **فِعالة here covers both crafts (صِناعة, زِراعة) and verbal nouns (كِتابة, دِراسة).** Is labelling it "craft or activity" in Beginner mode right for you?
3. **⚠ Form VIII is not regular for roots starting with ص, ض, ط, ظ, د, ذ or ز.** The تـ changes or merges (ص-ن-ع → اِصْطَنَعَ, not اِصْتَنَعَ). For the animation that's a "letters change on the way in" case, like the weak roots. My suggestion: grey those cells out in Phase 2 and bring them back in Phase 3 together with the weak roots.
4. **Form IX is used almost only for colours and physical defects**, so its column is entirely ✗. Keep the empty column as a lesson ("Form IX is for colours"), or hide it?
5. **◐ rare words:** show them (with a small "rare" tag) or grey them out?
6. **Roots:** swap any of the ten? Other sound candidates: ع-م-ل (work), ذ-ك-ر (remember), ح-م-د (praise), ن-ص-ر (help), ش-ك-ر (thank), ع-ب-د (worship).
