Wazn: Product Plan

Tap a root, pick a pattern, and watch the letters fly in to become a real word, then collect it.

Approved

Scope said → now we know

Scope said "collecting words from a root unlocks the next root." Now we know exactly what that means: 3 words unlocks the next root, every real word earns a gold seal, and the collection is word cards grouped by root, each showing the word, meaning and pattern.

Screens and behaviour

Look and feel

Looks like a manuscript, plays like a game. Calm and scholarly with calligraphic Arabic and gentle ornament; letters snap into place with satisfying feedback and small rewards. Easy for a teenager with no instructions. Arabic font: Amiri. In a formed word, root letters are ink and pattern letters are gold. No external reference yet.

States and edges

First use
  • Roots screen, Beginner mode, only ك-ت-ب unlocked, empty collection.
Empty collection
  • Invites them to open a root and form their first word.
Tapping a greyed-out pattern
  • Gentle shake plus "ك-ت-ب doesn't use this pattern in common Arabic." Nothing collected.
Quiz with too few words (Phase 2)
  • Unavailable until 3 words are collected, and says so. assumption
Returning
  • Unlocked roots, collection, seals and mode all restored.
All roots sealed
  • A completion moment on the Roots screen. assumption
Saved data cleared or unavailable
  • The app still works and starts fresh.

Your decisions

  • Roots first, no welcome screen: playing in one tap
  • English meaning is the one essential extra beside a word
  • 3 words unlock the next root; every real word earns a gold seal
  • Collection = word cards grouped by root
  • Greyed-out patterns shake and explain
  • Start in Beginner mode, visible switch
  • Quiz mixes "find the root" + "build the word", collected words only
  • Audio auto-plays once per word, then on tap
  • Cream paper, dark ink and gold

Deferred · Non-goals · Open questions

Deferred from the build
  • Accounts and cross-device sync
  • Browser text-to-speech fallback (after the hackathon)
  • Computer-generated words (only with checking, after the hackathon)
  • "Letters try and fall away" effect for greyed-out patterns (possible Phase 3 polish)
Non-goals
  • Not a dictionary: only the curated, verified word list
  • Not a full grammar course: roots and patterns only
  • No leaderboards or social features
Open questions
  • Your word list (roots, meanings, which cells are real): needed before Phase 1 build, can be prepared alongside the spec
  • Arabic font + keeping letter-joining during animation: spec step
  • Recording format for audio (Phase 3): spec step
